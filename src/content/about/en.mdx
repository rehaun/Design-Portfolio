<Section eyebrow="How I work" heading="Diagnose first, then design">

Most briefs I get are short — "make it better" — and the interesting part is figuring out what "better" actually means. I start by looking at how people really use the product: session recordings, quick usability checks with real users, conversations with the support and sales teams who hear from customers every day.

Once the problem is clear, I try to change as little as possible to fix it. Sometimes that's a new screen; sometimes it's removing a decision users never needed to make.

</Section>

<Section eyebrow="What I care about" heading="Designing with the whole team">

Good ideas often need someone else to make them real. I've learned to bring engineers and stakeholders in early, while the structure is still being decided — not after it's already approved — so the solution works for the people who build it as well as the people who use it.

</Section>
